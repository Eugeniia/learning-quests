2. Computer Vision (CV).
Trains computers to interpret & understand the visual world.
Types of CV: Classical (relies on pre-built libraries of featires) & Deep Learning (relies on CNNs).
Applications: Image Classification. Image Segmentation. Object Detection. Object Tracking. Image Generation. Edge Detection. Face Detection. Facial Recognition. Optical Character Recognition (OCR). Pattern Detection. Feature Matching.
Use cases: Skanska - CV for worker placement. Hypermile - AI-powered cruise control system for trucks. Cerrion - real-time monitoring of production lines using CV. Mashgin - self-checkout system for stores using CV. AI Cure - CV for medication adherence. Osprey - CV for oil well monitoring. Roaf - CV for waste sorting. Swiss Federal Institute of Technology - CV for stroke recovery. Cortexica - CV for workplace safety monitoring. Tomra - CV for automatic ore sorting.

3. Deep Learning (DL)
ML method based on artificail neural networks. The different ways that Neural Networks can be constructed are called Architectures. Finding the right Neural Network Architecture is actually a very creative process. Neural Networks have memory, which is effectively weights within these neurons. When performing the training part of a neural network, you have a lot of supervised data. Back propagation - adjusting the weights of each neuron. 
AI contains ML, ML contains CV, NLP, RL (Reinforcment Learning). DL overlaps with CV, NLP and RL. By adding DL, we have Deep CV, Deep NLP, Deep RL.
Use cases: Google AI - DL for cancer detection. CoCounsel - AI assistant that helps Legal Professionals using DL. GoldSpot Discoveries - DL for mineral exploration. Digital Domain - DL for visual effects (VFX). Ayasdi - DL for anti-money laundring. Deep Instinct - DL for cybersecurity. Doxel - DL for productivity tracking. PhotoRoom - Ai-powered photo editing platform using DL. Zestimate - DL for real estate prices. Zest Finance - DL for loan approval.

4. Reinforcement Learning (RL)
ML: Unsupervised Learning (UL), SUpervised Learning (SL), Reinforcement Learning (RL). 
Advantages of RL: 1) Has a greater scope of application than any form of SL. 2) Innovative unlike SL, which only imitates patterns in the original dataset. 3) Resistant to bias in the provided labeled dataset. 4) Able to learn online in real time. 5) Goal oriented. Can be used for sequences of actions. 6) Adaptable. Doesn't require retraining. 
Applications in Marketing: creating personalized recommendations; optimizing advirtizing budget; selecting the best content for advirtising; increasing customer lifetime value; predicting customer responses to price plan changes. 
Use cases: Intel - RL for semiconductor quality assurance. Google - RL for energy management. Decision Service - RL for advirtising. Trendyol - RL for email advertising. Optoro - RL in return management systems. Electa - RL for energy management. Mercedes-Benz drive pilot - RL for automated driving. Dynamic Coupon Targeting - RL for dynamically allocate online shopping coupons.

5. Robotics Process Automation (RPA)
RPA - the practice of using easily programable software to take care or handle highly repetitive, high-volume tasks.
Benefits: 1) RPA systems work 24/7 365 days a year. 2) RPA systems never get sick and never take days off. 3) RPA systems offer far more accuracy. 4) RPA systems offer far more speed to execute tasks. 5) RPA systems have inherent built-in tracking of metrics. 6) RPA systems are almost infinitely scalable. 7) RPA systems free up humans from repetitive tasks, increasing human morale. 8) RPA systems implementation result in amssive costs savings.
Risks: 1) Look RPA projects from am IT stand point of view. 2) Targeting the wrong processes. 3) Change management. 4) Automating too much. 5) Trusting only on the skills and not the experience of RPA consultant.
