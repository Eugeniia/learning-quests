### Agentic AI for Beginners: Build AI Agents with LangGraph
Evolution of AI systems: Conventional AI/Ml --> Deep Learning --> GenAI/LLM --> Agentic AI (Now) --> AGI (Artificaial Generative Intelligence - Future)

Agentic AI: goal-oriented, autonomous, multi-step planning, tool usage, adaptive.

Multi-agent architecture:
1) Supervisor agents - Act like a project manager. Deligate taks and ensure quality. Make decisions about which specialized agent should handle what.
2) Utility agents - Act like specialized employees (developers, testers, analysts). Each excels at one specific skill or function.
3) Orchestrator agents - Act like a delivery lead. Manage step-by-step workflows. Ensure tasks happen in the right order and track progress.

Balance automation with human oversight.
