## AI Fundamentals 2026: Safe AI Skills for Work
# Understanding Modern AI: Capabilities, Limits, and Real-World Impact
AI can be used for:
1) Enhancing products and services
2) Making new products and services
3) Automating what couldn't be automated before
4) Gaining new insight and intelligence

Three layers of applying AI in business
1) Outward facing (customer support systems, lead generation, appointment setting, mass outreach systems, qualification systems)
2) Day-to-day operations (co-pilits, knowledge retrival systems, knowledge bases, content generation, content repurposing, research, document drafting, data extraction from images/files, data management, staff training and onboarding, robotics)
3) Strategy, analysis, monitoring (research, ideation/brainstorming, automated monitoring, report creation, data analysis with intelligence, dashboards, business intelligence, sentiment analysis)

# Agents Explained
LLM = the "brain' that generates text based on patterns learned during training.
AI agent = rain + Hands. It is a LLM equipped with a planning engine and external tools. It breaks down complex goals into step-by-step actionable tasks. It can search databases, run code, and interact with APIs to execute the plan.

# AI Safety and Responsibility
Shadow AI is a form of Shadow IT using unapproved software. Shadow AI creates unseen risks and compliance gaps.
Cybercriminals used "WormGPT" - an AI model trained for malicious purposes, to generate high-quality pishing emails.
RAG (Retrieval Augumented Generation)
Intranet vs. Internet
Human-in-the-loop (A human reviews and approves the AI's work before it's used) vs human-on-the-loop (A human monitors periodically, stepping in when needed)

# Designing Effective Prompts
Be specific about your request & expectations. Task, limits, role and guidance.
Use emotion in the task.
Use a role.  e.g. "As, an expert in ...."
Share the context or provide examples (good and bad).
For complex tasks, break them down into sequential steps in the prompts.
Use templates.
Use iterative prompting for prompt adjustment.
Provide specific feedback.
Pseudonymisation.
